Ezra Msolla

I am a PhD Student in the Weinberg Institute at the University of Texas at Austin. My research centres around early universe cosmology, large-scale structure and statistical modelling of such complex physical systems.

I completed my Bachelor's degree at the University of Toronto, specializing in physics and astronomy, where I worked with Prof. Renée Hložek during my undergraduate thesis. Open areas of question I look to work towards include the microphysical signatures of dark matter, Inflationary cosmology, and the statistical modelling of large-scale data.

In addition to these scientific interests, outreach and mentorship remains a core principle in my career. I have supervised projects for both undergraduate and high school students and served as a teaching assistant at UT Austin. Apart from research and outreach, I enjoy the outdoors (hiking, football, basketball), cooking, and learning new languages.
